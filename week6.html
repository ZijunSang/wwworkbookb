RESEARCH JOURNAL

View reflection
This is a work I found online by an interactive art student called Joyce. The project was a “plant watering simulator,” where water acted as the input detected by an Arduino sensor, and the data then generated dynamic visual patterns in Processing. This was the first time I had seen an Arduino installation produce something beyond simple LEDs lighting up or buzzers making sound, but transforming an ordinary physical action into a digital response. The visuals were not simply decorative outputs, they felt alive and directly connected to the participant’s behaviour.
